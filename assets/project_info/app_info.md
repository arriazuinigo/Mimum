## PART 1: PRESENTATION / PITCH

### Opening — The Problem

We all share the most important moment of our lives, being born. But behind that moment is a woman whose heart was put under extraordinary pressure to bring us here. And in too many cases, no one is watching to end afterwards.

More than one in ten pregnancies in the UK suffer from complications. This can increase up to five times the risk of cardiovascular diseases for a woman for the lifetime. Yet, 96% of them doesn't know about it.

### Three Gaps in Healthcare

We identified three main gaps in how our healthcare system addresses this issue:

1. **Clinical gap** — the medical link between pregnancy complications and cardiovascular risk is under-recognized.
2. **Emotional gap** — mental health (anxiety, stress, postpartum mental health challenges) is highly linked with cardiovascular disease but is not taken into account in long-term follow-up.
3. **System gap** — conditions such as pre-eclampsia are strong predictors of cardiovascular disease, but usually they are lost after pregnancy. As a result, we lose the opportunity to prevent it.

### Introducing MIMO

MIMO is a way of making monitoring easier and turning healthcare data into actionable insights.

We integrate a blood pressure monitor into MIMO, our weighted pillow to reduce anxiety on those days where everything feels overwhelming. The user will use MIMO to take the measurement. Our software will just collect the data and analyze it. Blood pressure is the strongest indicator for cardiovascular disease, but can be complemented with weight, medication, insulin, and mental health care.

Our product is built around MIMO and our app. After a one-time connection between those, the app becomes the central hub where the user can view, collect, and better understand their healthcare data.

Everything starts with a few general questions: Where is the woman in her journey? How was her pregnancy? After that, we can create a personalized pathway for the long-term follow-up.

### Prototype

We aim to create our own customized weighted pillow, and buy the blood pressure device. We will assemble it together so we don't have to deal with regulatory issues. But we never want to lose sight of our main mission: we want to reduce the amount of women who suffer from cardiovascular diseases because action came too late.

### Three-Step Roadmap

1. **Step 1** — already integrated in the first phase of the program: each woman will be able to monitor herself at home with MIMO.
2. **Step 2** — compute a cardiovascular risk score, turning the data collected into actionable insights for healthcare professionals.
3. **Step 3** — collaboration with hospitals and healthcare providers to integrate MIMO into clinical workflows, giving clinicians the opportunity to know their patients' data before and take action.

### Business Model

B2C business model with two main revenue streams:

- **MIMO weighted pillow** — priced at 100 euros.
- **Premium subscription** — priced at 350 euros (monthly subscription offering a much more personalized experience).

Compounding effect: every month, more women; every month, more data; every month, more revenue.

We're expecting good potential growth, but we want to remain realistic and scalable.

### Funding Ask

We are seeking **200,000 euros** to bring MIMO to the first woman who needs it most. This will help us in finishing the development of our product, validating it, building our infrastructure, and marketing it.

### Team & Closing

This is the team who made this possible. And without them, this wouldn't be possible.

This problem has been ignored for decades. We are choosing not to do it anymore. And we hope you do the same choice.
